About Me

I'm a climate researcher passionate about bridging atmospheric science and renewable energy applications. My work focuses on understanding how large-scale climate modes—particularly the Madden-Julian Oscillation—influence subseasonal wind and solar energy variability over East Asia.

Using ERA5 reanalysis data, GloSea6 dynamical forecasts, and advanced statistical methods, I aim to develop predictable signals that can improve renewable energy forecasting and grid operations.

Research Focus
